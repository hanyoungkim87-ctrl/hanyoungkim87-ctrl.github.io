DESIGN NOTES

What this demo compares

01

A shared starting point

In every condition, participants select battery life and sound quality as instructed. Products, prices, and the initial recommendation are identical, with default weights of 50:50.

02

Explanation and control

No explanation shows the recommendation alone. Static explanation discloses the weights. Explanation + control also lets participants change those weights and receive an updated recommendation.

03

A responsive recommendation

75:25 → A, 50:50 → B, 25:75 → C. This rule-based simulator ranks products by the weighted sum of their displayed scores. It is not a live AI service.

For research design review. No responses are collected or transmitted. Surveys, random assignment, and participant identification are not included. The interactive condition requires participants to change and apply the weights at least once before finishing. Any product can be selected in every condition.
