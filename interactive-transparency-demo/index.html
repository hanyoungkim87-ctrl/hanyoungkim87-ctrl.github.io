DESIGN NOTES

What this demo compares

01

A shared starting point

Everyone copies and pastes the same assigned shopping request. All conditions show the same products in A–B–C order, the same prices, and the same initial recommendation: Product B.

02

Explanation, then source control

No explanation shows the product recommendations alone. Static and interactive conditions share the same initial source explanation and 50:50 weights. Only the interactive condition adds an optional slider and Update recommendations button.

03

A responsive source-weight model

The two inputs are request match and overall product evaluation. Battery and sound ratings trade off across products. Request weights of 75%, 50%, and 25% recommend A, B, and C. Any product can be selected without adjusting the weights.

Design review demo · v2026-10-21-study-v4. Products and ratings are fictional. Battery is scored at one point per hour, capped at 10. Request match averages battery and sound; overall evaluation averages all four displayed attributes. No live reviews, free-text interpretation, or AI model are used. The initial recommendation is B; request weights of 75%, 50%, and 25% recommend A, B, and C. This is a three-condition design.
